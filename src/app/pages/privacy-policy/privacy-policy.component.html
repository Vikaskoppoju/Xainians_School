Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through our admission enquiry, contact, and parent login forms — including student and parent names, contact details, and academic documents uploaded for admission purposes.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected is used solely to process admission enquiries, respond to contact requests, manage parent-portal access, and share relevant school communications such as circulars and notices.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Data may be shared with CBSE or government authorities where required for regulatory compliance.

4. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect the data you share with us, including restricted access to student records.

5. Cookies

Our website may use minimal cookies for essential functionality. We do not use third-party tracking cookies for advertising.

6. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, write to us at info@xainiansschool.edu.in.
