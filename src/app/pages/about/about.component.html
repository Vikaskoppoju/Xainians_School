Our History

A Legacy of Learning Since 2000

Founded in the town of Anantnag, Xainians International School began with a simple vision — to give children of the Valley access to world-class education without leaving home. From a handful of classrooms, we have grown into a full-fledged LKG–XII campus at Batengoo, known for academic rigor, cultural richness, and a nurturing environment.

Today, we are proud to be a CBSE-affiliated institution trusted by thousands of families across Anantnag and the surrounding districts.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide holistic, values-based education that blends academic excellence with international teaching methods, preparing students to thrive as responsible global citizens.

🔭 Our Vision

To be the region's most trusted center of learning — where every child discovers their potential in a safe, inclusive and technology-enabled environment.
