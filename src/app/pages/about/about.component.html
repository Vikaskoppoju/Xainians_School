Our History

17 Years of Standing Up for the Students

The name Saint Xain's descended like a potion from Hazrat Zain-ud-Din Wali (R.A.), one of the most esteemed and loved Muslim saints of the 5th Century Kashmir. Although the chill of November 2007 was letting everything freeze and come to a still, the passion of contributing to society kept breathing warm in the hearts of its founders — eventually materializing this dream into reality in the Civil Lines area (K.P. Road) of Anantnag.

Like an unfledged bird, the school took its flight with just 4 to 5 teachers and 13 students. Today it flies as a "Shaheen" above all others as far as quality education is concerned — now shining from our campus on the National Highway near Peaks Auto, Uranhall, Anantnag, as the torch-bearer of quality education in South Kashmir.

🎯 Our Mission

Saint Xain's International School sees every student as a successful torch-bearer of high value, intelligence and principles. We target excellence in every field — Academics, Sports, Fine Arts and more — believing in exploring the latent talent of our students and giving them wings to fly high above the daily trifles.

🔭 Our Vision

We take a pledge to empower our students with value-based education and to enhance essential skills that will support them throughout their lives — stimulating creative and critical faculties that help them embark on a mission of life-long learning and contribute to a positive change in the society they live in.
